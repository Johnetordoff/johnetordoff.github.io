How to link grids
Choose Portal, tap its first cell, select another grid, and tap its partner. Each portal shows ▦ and its destination grid number. A missing partner turns red and shattered. Select a grid and Delete, then type yes to confirm its exact number. Grid numbers are never reused within a pattern.
